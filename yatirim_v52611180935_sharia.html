What the analyst argues

After peaking at 124.00 on 3 September, the bank corrected to about 83.00, then rose for three straight weeks and confirmed a new weekly pivot low. The call is BUY with targets at 120, 125 and 130 and a stop at 102.

Honest reading: the short-term trend is sideways, the medium-term trend is up, and the 5-day RSI above 70 says a short pause is likely. The close sits just under the first resistance at 111.89. Buying near 108.61 gives a 6.6% stop distance and a decent reward to 120, but waiting for a pullback toward 100–103 would improve the ratio further.
